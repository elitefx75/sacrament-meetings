This week I added authentication for the ward administrator. The create-meeting
route and meeting edit routes are protected, while public meeting pages remain
available without signing in. I also added site metadata, including a default
title and title template, a description, and Open Graph and Twitter metadata
for sharing.

One challenge was protecting only the management routes without blocking
public access to meetings. I resolved this in the NextAuth authorization
callback by matching the create route and meeting edit paths, and redirecting
signed-in users away from the login page.

I used AI to help understand and implement the authentication and metadata
patterns, and to review the route protection logic. I checked the resulting
configuration against the application routes to make sure public meeting pages
were still accessible.
