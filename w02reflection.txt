 architectural or component decision
 I chose to split the meeting agenda into reusable components 
 inside MeetingDetail, such as AgendaRow, AgendaList, and SpeakerList,
instead of placing all of the markup directly in the page. I considered 
creating one large component or using a generic configuration-driven renderer, 
but smaller purpose-specific components were clearer and easier to maintain while 
still avoiding repeated layout code. This also made it easier to handle different 
data types, such as single values, lists, and speakers, consistently.
